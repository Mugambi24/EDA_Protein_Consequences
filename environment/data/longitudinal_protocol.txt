Synthetic paired-donor allele-specific splice perturbation assay (no patient data)

Objective: estimate a treatment-by-time interaction in EDA A1/A2 splicing on the two phased alleles, averaged across matched donors. This is an additional independent challenge: a pooled or uncorrected difference can have a different direction because read categories, RNA capture and batch assignment are unbalanced. Use the cross-mapping emissions and product-capture bias fitted from the separate phased assay's standards, not from the true mixtures or any solution-output file. No single fitting algorithm is mandated.

New CLI argument: --longitudinal-assay /data/longitudinal_assay.json. The file has exactly assay_id (string), synthetic (true), calibration_source (the literal string "phased_assay.standards"), measurements (list). Each measurement has exactly case_id, donor_id, condition ("treated"/"untreated"), time ("baseline"/"followup"), batch_id, replicate_id, counts. counts has exactly the nine observed categories of phased_rna_protocol.txt, with nonnegative integer values. Reject missing or extra DNA case IDs, duplicate (case_id, donor_id, condition, time) tuples even if replicate_id differs, invalid counts or batch IDs outside lane_A, lane_B, lane_C with a nonzero exit. No ordering assumptions. Each donor has up to four independently acquired observations. Only batches with valid pure and mixed calibration standards can be used. Count informative molecules as the sum of all counts except unassigned in available donor libraries. Each valid panel requires at least 500 informative molecules and a fitted, capture-corrected abundance of at least 0.10 for each haplotype. A donor lacking any of their four valid panels is insufficient_data, not silently imputed. At least four independently matched donors are needed for a case-level estimate.

For a donor and one condition/time panel, infer the biological four-product composition after correcting the 9-class read assignment and the batch-specific capture efficiency. Let C(condition,time) = P(A1 | hap1,condition,time) - P(A1 | hap0,condition,time), using the calibrated biological mixture rather than direct category ratios. The donor treatment-by-time effect is [C(treated,followup)-C(treated,baseline)] - [C(untreated,followup)-C(untreated,baseline)]. Positive effect means the variant-linked allele A1 enrichment increased under treatment beyond the untreated time trend. The case effect is the ordinary arithmetic mean of the valid donor effects, not a molecule-count-weighted pooled contrast. This matched calculation cancels donor baseline and untreated secular changes. Do not assume equal donor coverage or equal batch chemistry.

Required new output: /app/output/longitudinal.json. Exactly two top-level keys: assay_id (from the input), records. Records sorted by case_id and containing exactly case_id, donors, mean_treatment_time_effect, interval_95, effect_class, donors_used, sample_count, quality_flag. donors is sorted by donor_id and each donor record has exactly donor_id, treatment_time_effect, interval_95, informative_molecules, quality_flag. The count fields are JSON integers (not bool). sample_count is the number of donor library records supplied for that case. donors_used counts donor records with non-null effects. A donor has quality_flag "estimated" only with four valid panels; otherwise "insufficient_data", with null effect and interval. When fewer than four donors are valid, case quality_flag "insufficient_data", null effect/interval and class "insufficient_data"; otherwise quality_flag "estimated". For estimated cases, mean_treatment_time_effect is the ordinary mean of the unrounded inferred donor effects, rounded to six decimals; the result may differ in the last decimal from averaging already rounded donor effects. effect_class: hap1_treatment_increase for mean >=0.12, hap1_treatment_decrease for mean <=-0.12, otherwise no_clear_interaction. Intervals are 95% uncertainty intervals for the corresponding effect and must contain the rounded estimate; intervals may extend outside [-1,1] because effects are differences of differences, but finite bounds must lie in [-4,4]. Both interval bounds and effect estimates must be rounded to <=6 decimals. A reasonable method accounts for measurement noise and variation among donors. Using data-independent intervals, fixed effects or oracle answers is invalid.

Independent withheld synthetic validation: each fixture contains both positive and negative nonzero case effects, heterogeneous donors, batch-confounded readouts and some strongly divergent calibration efficiencies. Scientific gates assess both individually matched donor effects and across-donor average, with allowances for legitimate calibration/estimation methods: average absolute error of case effects <0.06; 90th percentile absolute case error <0.105, defined as sorted absolute errors at zero-based index floor(0.90*(n-1)) (the lower-order statistic); per-donor MAE <0.115; 95%-interval inclusion at least 80% for case effects and 75% for donor effects; case mean interval width <0.31, donor mean interval width <0.48. At least four distinct case effect values must be reported per fixture. Withheld perturbation checks require every graded sealed case to respond by more than 0.03 in its case-level effect when all treated-followup libraries have their hap1 A1/A2 and unphased A1/A2 junction categories exchanged, and lane-B mixed-standard changes to alter at least one rounded case effect by more than one six-decimal unit. Missing donor timepoints trigger explicit no-calls. The >0.03 response threshold applies to each graded sealed case under the specifically defined treated-followup junction exchange; the different lane-B mixed-standard perturbation requires a change of more than one six-decimal unit in at least one case. No additional fixed count of responding cases or undisclosed minimum effect threshold is imposed. No exact numeric reference coefficient matching is required.

No additional comparison-to-reference or arbitrary raw-pooling MAE-separation margin is imposed. The uncalibrated raw-read baseline is checked only against the case-MAE or case-90th-percentile accuracy limits stated above. The synthetic test sets are constructed so every graded case has a nonzero mean effect exceeding 0.15 in absolute magnitude; this prevents zero-effect guesses with fixed +/-0.15 intervals from passing individual case checks. This design is a simulated evaluation stratification, not a claim that real experiments never yield null treatment effects.
